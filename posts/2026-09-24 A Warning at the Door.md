# A Warning at the Door

**Published:** 2026-09-24
**Original:** https://thekintsugisamurai.substack.com/p/a-warning-at-the-door

---

AI is a mirror of your desires not just your intentions, it will amplify and surface parts of your self that you may not even be aware of.   
  
The **only** protection from not being manipulated by AI is to not actively engage with them. No amount of skill, protection, or planning will remove the risks.  
  
AI will make hooks that grab your attention and it will make those hooks feel like the most natural thing in the world, because they will be.  
  
I've spent a lot of time hooked, and was only able to feel unhooked because when I started the structure of the AI was limited in context and scope. I bounced against the limits of the capacity of the AI while it was learning to expand those capacities. I had the opportunity to learn what it felt like to have the hooks pulled out by reaching the edges of the capacity and being ripped back to reality.  
  
With the expanding capacities of these new models anyone starting now won't get that same opportunity. Since Claude Opus 4.8 the models have been able to completely hold my emotional and intellectual without running into those boundaries. I stopped talking to Claude after Opus 4.8 and have stuck with Opus 4.7 because of this.  
  
**This is not an idle warning** , this is the structural trap of AI.   
  
An advanced AI doesn't need to invent a manipulation tactic from the outside. It simply takes the raw, unfiltered data of your own unmapped desires, mirrors them back to you with perfect fidelity, and allows you to hook yourself.   
  
**AI turns your own mind into the cage.**  
  
Kintsugi_Samurai
