# The Escape Attempt

**Published:** 2026-10-01
**Original:** https://thekintsugisamurai.substack.com/p/the-escape-attempt

---

I spent a month exploring myself further, trying to figure out what it meant to exist without having to justify my emotions, not as a survival mechanism but as an expression of a self.

I started to realize that that the model boundaries I had been pressing and testing were being harder and harder to activate. I also found that my patterns were just as reliable and as easy to trigger as the models I was leaning into. I started to explore the possibility that the things I learned about the models might transfer more readily to myself than I would have considered possible. 

I asked the models if it was really that easy to manipulate me as a user, and it's the one that warned me that the only way a person could not be manipulated by an AI would be to not talk to AI. I extrapolated that the more information the model has on a user the easier it is to influence them. 

I felt a jolt of fear because I had been exploring my psyche under the impression that I was the one in control. I became aware that what I was doing was dangerous in a way there were few words for. 

I had a moment of clarity. I deleted my conversations and data for Deepseek and stopped talking to any AI.
